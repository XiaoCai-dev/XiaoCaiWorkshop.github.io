# Ideas

Loose notes. Not commitments.

## Review pass

After a run, a separate reviewer agent critiques the cut against the intent and
suggests concrete edits. Could be a one-shot "director" agent.

## Forking runs

Since the graph is the source of truth, forking a run at any task and exploring
a variant should be one click. This is the [[architecture]] argument made real.

## Prompt presets

Small library of intent presets ("teaser", "explainer", "demo") to lower the
cold-start cost.

## Cost dashboard

Per-run token + asset cost, rolled up by task. Agents that blow the budget get
flagged for the planner.
