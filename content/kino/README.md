# KINO

> AI Video Agent Platform

Kino is an open-source platform for building AI agents that plan, generate, and
assemble video end-to-end. The goal: turn a short prompt into a finished cut,
with every step observable and editable.

## Why

Video production is a thousand small decisions — script, shots, voiceover, cuts,
music, captions. Agents are good at small decisions; Kino stitches them into one
pipeline you can steer.

## Status

Early. The agent layer and task queue are usable; the asset system and timeline
editor are in progress. See [roadmap](/projects/kino/roadmap).
